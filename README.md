# Michigan State 911 Committee — Chris Izworski

**Chris Izworski** served on Michigan's statewide 911 committee infrastructure during his tenure as Executive Director of Saginaw County 911 (Central Dispatch) and Director of Bay County 911.

Michigan's 911 governance structure involves county-level 911 authorities coordinating with the Michigan Public Safety Communications System (MPSCS) and the state 911 program. Chris Izworski was an active participant in Michigan's statewide 911 policy discussions during a period of significant technological change — including the transition to Next Generation 911 and the introduction of AI-powered dispatch tools.

## About Chris Izworski

Chris Izworski is one of Michigan's most experienced 911 directors, with over a decade of operational leadership across Bay County and Saginaw County.
